# Hi! I'm Ankit


Hi! Welcome to my blog. I'm Ankit, a research scientist at Fujitsu Research. My main areas of interest are in deep learning. I have worked in different areas of deep learning at different times - computer vision in my masters, NLP in my internship and more recently I've begun applying RL algorithms to some open problems in quantum computing. Why quantum computing? Because during my PhD I found that many low level problems in quantum computing (i.e. actually making algorithms work on quantum computers) are optimization problems in disguise. I've spent the past couple of years developing ML algorithms to solve these optimization problems in QC. However, just to be clear, my interests are primarily in deep learning and RL.

I am a voracious reader who primarily reads history, biographies, scifi and spy thrillers. In my spare time, I like to solve algorithmic puzzles and box (middleweight). 

Feel free to reach out if you like my posts. I write for myself mostly, but I would be glad to know if my posts helped spark your interest in my fields of expertise.
